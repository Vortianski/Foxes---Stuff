# Foxes & Stuff
A Minecraft mod that makes foxes more useful, customizable, and fun. Tame foxes, equip them with special armor, give them items to carry, and feed them Golden Berries to change their species.

## Features

<details>
<summary><strong>Taming Foxes</strong></summary>

Foxes can now be tamed, allowing you to keep them as companions and take care of them just like other tameable animals.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Fox Armor</strong></summary>

Tamed foxes can wear special armor designed specifically for them.

Armor provides your fox with additional protection while also giving them a unique appearance.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Fox Items</strong></summary>

Tamed foxes can hold items in their mouths.

Give your fox almost any item and let it carry it around with you. Perfect for giving your companion something useful, decorative, or just silly to hold.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Golden Berries</strong></summary>

Golden Berries can be fed to tamed foxes to change their species.

Different fox species can have their own appearance, allowing you to customize your companion without needing to find another fox.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Fluff</strong></summary>

Foxes & Stuff adds additional rendering effects to make foxes look fluffier and more expressive.

If you prefer the vanilla look or want to improve performance, **Fluff** can be disabled in the mod configuration.

</details>
